### Notes on redistribution

- **Macaulay Library** assets are copyrighted by the recordist. Their terms
  permit use for research and education with attribution, but do not permit
  relicensing. Cite each asset by its ML number and recordist, and confirm
  that redistribution of the audio file itself — rather than a link to it —
  is permitted for your use. If it is not, replace the files with a script
  that downloads them by asset ID.
- **Xeno-Canto** recordings each carry their own Creative Commons licence.
  An `NC` or `SA` term travels with the file and constrains how this archive
  may be distributed, so check XC609336 specifically before including it.
- Whatever is resolved here should also appear in the Fig. 3 caption, so
  readers of the paper can trace each recording.
